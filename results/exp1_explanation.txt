EXPERIMENT 1 — EXPLORATION STRATEGIES
======================================
We compare two epsilon-decay schedules (deliverable: >=2):

1. Exponential decay: ε = ε × 0.995 each episode.
   Fast early decay, long gentle tail.
   Best for environments where good routes are discovered quickly.

2. Linear decay: ε = ε - (1/N) each episode.
   Steady, predictable reduction.
   Gives equal exploration time to all episodes proportionally.

Theory justification (from lecture):
High epsilon early = exploration (agent knows nothing).
Low epsilon later  = exploitation (agent uses what it learned).
The optimal schedule decays fast enough to converge but slow
enough that every reachable state gets visited early.
